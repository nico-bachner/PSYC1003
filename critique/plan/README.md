## Assessment requirements

- Write a **750–1,000-word** critique of **one** supplied video, worth **25%** of the course mark.
- Identify the speaker’s main argument, evaluate scientific evidence for and against it, and reach an evidence-based judgement.
- Discuss the potential benefits or harms of the video for an audience without a scientific background.
- Use published scientific literature, with approximately **5–10 references** as a practical guide. Websites and blogs are not appropriate scientific evidence.
- Use professional, objective language rather than “I think” or “I feel.” Agreement, disagreement, and a qualified verdict are all permitted.

Source: [Essay instructions](../assessment/essay_instructions.md).

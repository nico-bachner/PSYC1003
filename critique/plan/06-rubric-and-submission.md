The [critique rubric](../assessment/1003-critique-rubric.pdf) allocates:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Introduction | 15% |
| Thesis | 5% |
| Topic/evidence selection | 15% |
| Argument | 20% |
| Analysis | 15% |
| Communication | 10% |
| Conclusion | 10% |
| Referencing | 10% |

Argument, evidence selection, and analysis together account for half the mark. Prioritise relevant evidence, accurate explanations of methods and findings, and a coherent evaluation of both sides. Do not imply that a paper was the speaker’s actual source unless this can be established.

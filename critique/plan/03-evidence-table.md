# 3. Build the evidence table

[Plan index](README.md)

Maintain a separate `critique/research/evidence_table.md` file using **one structured entry per paper**, followed by a compact comparison table. Detailed findings and limitations belong in the entries rather than wide table cells. This format suits the expected 5–10 sources; consider a spreadsheet only if sorting and filtering a larger collection becomes useful.

Keep what the researchers found separate from our interpretation. A study may support a narrow claim while weakening the speaker’s broader conclusion. For Video 1, distinguish reported memory gaps, a protective blocking explanation, and the accuracy of later recollections; do not preselect the scientific verdict.

## Structure for each paper

- **Full reference and DOI:** authors, year, title, journal, volume, issue, pages or article number, and DOI; note publication status where relevant.
- **Design, sample and measures:** include the relevant comparison or control.
- **Reported findings:** record the relevant results with page or section locators.
- **Limitations:** distinguish limitations reported by the authors from our appraisal.
- **Our interpretation:** explain whether the paper supports, contradicts or qualifies a specific part of the claim, and why.
- **Planned essay use:** identify the paragraph or section and the point the source would support.

## Compact comparison table

Use one short row per paper and link the source label to its detailed entry within the evidence file.

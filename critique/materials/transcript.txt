Why can't you remember if something traumatic happened to you? We get this question a lot as EMDR therapists and the reason usually is because it's protective. If something is so upsetting to the nervous system and it overwhelms your ability to cope, then what the brain does is it blocks access to that memory.

While it's helpful at the time, it can be unhelpful if you're trying to remember something or you feel like something happened to you but you can't remember. There are a lot of techniques when working with that type of dissociative amnesia that can really help you feel safe. Either those memories will come back or they won't, and you can process the residual effects so that you could still relieve your symptoms of anxiety and depression and just go on and live your best life.

# Critique essay project checklist

**Personal completion target:** Thursday, 1 October 2026, 11:00 pm Sydney time (AEST).

**Submission deadline:** Friday, 9 October 2026, 11:59 pm Sydney time (AEDT).

## Preparation

- [x] Read the assignment instructions and rubric.
- [x] Organise the preparation plan into [modules](plan/README.md).
- [x] Review lecture materials and compare videos — [Video 1 recommended](research/background/lecture_topic_selection.md).
- [x] Agree to draft in Markdown, with the [finalisation workflow](plan/05-document-and-references.md) recorded in the plan.
- [x] Confirm the deadline, personal target, and accepted formats: PDF, DOC, DOCX.
- [x] Select Video 1 and record its central claim and initial scope — [topic selection](plan/01-topic-selection.md).

## Research and argument

- [x] Find and read scientific evidence supporting and challenging the claim — [reviewed research and access limits](research/review/review_audit.md).
- [x] Complete the evidence table and select approximately 5–10 references — [eight-source shortlist](research/reviewed_shortlist.md) and [evidence table](research/evidence_table.md).
- [x] Develop a proposed evidence-based thesis and paragraph outline — [synthesis](research/thesis_synthesis.md) and [argument plan](plan/04-argument-outline.md).
- [ ] Review the proposed thesis with the user; it is not yet user-approved.

## Draft and revise

- [ ] Write the full Markdown draft, targeting about 920 words.
- [ ] Evaluate both sides, explain the overall verdict, and discuss audience benefits or harms.
- [ ] Revise for accurate evidence use, clear reasoning, and professional scientific writing.
- [ ] Verify APA 7 citations and references against the original sources.

## Finalise and submit

- [ ] Choose the final production method and submission format — [document setup](plan/05-document-and-references.md).
- [ ] Add the required cover page and apply the required font and double spacing.
- [ ] Confirm 750–1,000 words, including citations and subheadings but excluding cover and references.
- [ ] Check the rubric and visually inspect every page of the final file.
- [ ] Finish the submission-ready essay by the personal target.
- [ ] Upload to Canvas, verify the uploaded file, and save the receipt — [submission details](plan/06-rubric-and-submission.md).

Use this checklist for project milestones and the linked modules for detailed tasks. Mark items complete only when their outcomes are verified.

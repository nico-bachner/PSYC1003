It's Mental Health Awareness Month so this month I have to talk about serotonin because low serotonin does not always look like sadness. As a nurse practitioner low serotonin often shows up as:
- anxiety
- rumination
- obsessive thoughts
- irritability
- poor sleep
- low mood
- overthinking
- emotionally feeling overwhelmed
One of the biggest signs I see is people say, "I can't shut my brain off." This is what's happening: serotonin helps regulate mood, sleep, emotional stability, appetite, and anxiety.
Here's what most people don't realize: your lifestyle directly impacts your serotonin levels and that includes:
- poor sleep
- chronic stress
- inflammation, whether it's chronic disease or acute inflammation
- your stomach is off
- your gut health is suffering
- low protein intake
- too much alcohol
- constant overstimulation
All of these things, in isolation or together, matter. As a nurse practitioner I wish more people understood that mental health is not just emotional; it's truly rooted in physiology.

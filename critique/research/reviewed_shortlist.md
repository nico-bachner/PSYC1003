The reviewed evidence supports taking memory gaps and some accurate delayed recollections seriously, and provides laboratory evidence for deliberate retrieval control. It does **not establish that protective blocking is the usual cause** of uncertainty about a possible traumatic event. This is the current evidence assessment, not a claim that such blocking is impossible or a completed essay thesis. The final argumentative emphasis remains open for the outline stage.

## Remaining evidence gaps and safeguards

- **Frequency and mechanism:** no reviewed design estimates the relative causes of gaps among people unsure an event occurred. Neither “usually protective blocking” nor “usually false memory” follows from this set.
- **Measurement:** clinical reports and documented-event nonreport do not continuously measure whether a memory was encoded, inaccessible, undisclosed, forgotten normally or reinterpreted. Recovery history is often retrospective.
- **Generalisation:** laboratory suppression of new material does not reproduce automatic loss of an autobiographical trauma; the observed trauma-history and PTSD group differences are not interchangeable.
- **Accuracy:** corroboration can support event occurrence without validating every detail or establishing the earlier forgetting mechanism. Absence of corroboration cannot establish falsity.
- **Search limits:** this was targeted course-led searching and citation tracing through accessible originals, publisher/DOI records and university resources, not a preregistered systematic review or a comprehensive PsycINFO/library search. Goodman full text and the Dalenberg (2014) reply remain useful optional follow-ups.
- **Audience effects:** the survey establishes beliefs and self-reports, not the effect of this particular video. Any proposed benefit or harm must be framed as a plausible implication, not a measured outcome.
- **Fairness to the speaker:** acknowledge that memories may not return and that the speaker allows symptom improvement without recovery. Do not attribute a guarantee of accuracy, explicit suggestive methods or an assertion about all trauma therapies to them.
